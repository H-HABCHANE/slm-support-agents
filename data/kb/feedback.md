# NovaShop – Feedback

## Complaints (complaint)
Complaints are handled by a human agent. They are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 10 business days. If the customer is not satisfied with the answer, they can contact the NovaShop mediation service.

## Reviews (review)
Customers can leave a product review from Account > Orders after delivery, or a review of the service from the link in the delivery email. Reviews are published after moderation within 48 hours.

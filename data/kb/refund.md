# NovaShop – Refunds and returns

## Refund policy (check_refund_policy)
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Exceptions: custom-made products, opened software and hygiene products cannot be returned. Return shipping is free for NovaShop Plus members and costs 4.90 EUR otherwise.

## Get a refund (get_refund)
Start a return in Account > Orders > Return an item and print the prepaid label. The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Refunds for defective items also cover the original shipping cost.

## Track a refund (track_refund)
Refund status is shown in Account > Orders > Returns. Once issued, a card refund can take 3 to 10 business days to appear on the bank statement, depending on the bank. If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, contact customer service with the order number.

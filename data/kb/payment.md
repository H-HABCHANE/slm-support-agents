# NovaShop – Payment

## Payment methods (check_payment_methods)
NovaShop accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and bank transfer. Payment in 3 instalments without fees is available for orders above 150 EUR. Cash on delivery is not available.

## Payment issues (payment_issue)
If a payment is declined, check the card details and 3-D Secure validation with the bank, then try again or use another method. A pending charge for a failed payment is released automatically by the bank within 7 days. If the customer was charged twice for the same order, the duplicate is refunded automatically within 3 business days; customer service can confirm it with the order number.

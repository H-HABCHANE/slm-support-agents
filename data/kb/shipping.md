# NovaShop – Shipping address

## Set up a shipping address (set_up_shipping_address)
Shipping addresses are managed in Account > Addresses. Up to 5 addresses can be saved, and one can be set as default. Pickup points are selected at checkout.

## Change the shipping address (change_shipping_address)
The shipping address of an order can be changed in Account > Orders while the status is "Received". After the order is "Preparing" or "Shipped", the address cannot be changed by NovaShop; for a shipped parcel the customer can use the carrier's redirection link in the tracking email.

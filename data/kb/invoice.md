# NovaShop – Invoices

## Get an invoice (get_invoice)
Invoices are available as PDF in Account > Orders > Download invoice once the order has shipped. Guest customers receive the invoice by email after shipping. Business customers can add a company name and VAT number before checkout; invoices cannot be re-issued with a different company name after payment.

## Check an invoice (check_invoice)
Each invoice shows the order number, items, VAT and payment method. If an amount looks wrong, compare it with the order confirmation email; partial shipments produce one invoice per package. Invoice errors must be reported within 60 days.

# NovaShop – Delivery

## Delivery options (delivery_options)
Three options are available in France: Standard (4.90 EUR, free above 50 EUR), Express (9.90 EUR) and Pickup point (2.90 EUR). International delivery is available to EU countries only.

## Delivery period (delivery_period)
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, Express 1 to 2 business days, and Pickup point 3 to 4 business days. Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. A parcel not delivered 5 business days after the expected date is investigated by customer service.

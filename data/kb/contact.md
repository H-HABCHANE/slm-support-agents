# NovaShop – Contact

## Contact customer service (contact_customer_service)
Customer service is available by chat and at +33 1 23 45 67 89, Monday to Friday from 9 am to 6 pm (Paris time). Email requests through the contact form are answered within 24 business hours.

## Talk to a human agent (contact_human_agent)
Customers can ask the chatbot for a human agent at any time. During opening hours a human agent joins the chat in less than 10 minutes; outside opening hours a ticket is created and a human agent replies the next business day.

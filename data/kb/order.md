# NovaShop – Orders

## Place an order (place_order)
Add items to the cart and check out. Orders can be placed as a guest or with an account. An order confirmation email with the order number (format NS-XXXXXX) is sent immediately.

## Change an order (change_order)
Items, quantities and options can be changed in Account > Orders while the order status is "Received". Once the status is "Preparing" or later, the order can no longer be changed; cancel it (if possible) and place a new one.

## Track an order (track_order)
Order status is visible in Account > Orders or with the tracking link in the shipping email. Statuses are: Received, Preparing, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered. Guest customers can track with the order number and email at novashop.example/track.

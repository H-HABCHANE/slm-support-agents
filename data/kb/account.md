# NovaShop – Account help

## Create an account (create_account)
Customers create an account at novashop.example/signup with an email address and a password of at least 10 characters. A confirmation email is sent within 5 minutes; the link is valid for 24 hours. One email address can only be linked to one account.

## Registration problems (registration_problems)
If the confirmation email does not arrive, check the spam folder, then use "Resend confirmation" on the sign-up page. The message "email already in use" means an account already exists: use password recovery instead. Sign-up is blocked after 5 failed attempts for 30 minutes.

## Edit account details (edit_account)
Name, phone number and email can be changed in Account > Profile. Changing the email requires confirming the new address. Date of birth can only be changed by customer service.

## Switch account (switch_account)
To switch between a personal and a business account, log out and log in with the other account. Orders cannot be moved from one account to another.

## Delete an account (delete_account)
Account deletion is requested in Account > Privacy > Delete my account. It is permanent and takes effect after 14 days, during which it can be cancelled. Accounts with an order in progress or an open refund cannot be deleted until it is closed. Invoices are kept for 10 years for legal reasons.

## Recover a password (recover_password)
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter the account email. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. For security, NovaShop agents never ask for a password.

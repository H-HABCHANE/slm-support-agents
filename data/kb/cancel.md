# NovaShop – Cancellations

## Cancel an order (cancel_order)
An order can be cancelled free of charge in Account > Orders > Cancel while its status is "Received" or "Preparing". Once the order is "Shipped", it cannot be cancelled: the customer must wait for delivery and request a return.

## Cancellation fees (check_cancellation_fee)
There is no fee for cancelling an order before shipping. Custom-made products (engraving, made-to-measure) carry a cancellation fee of 15 % of the item price once production has started. Annual NovaShop Plus subscriptions cancelled after the 14-day withdrawal period are not refunded for the current year.
